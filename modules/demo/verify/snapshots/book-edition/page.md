## This Book Only Covers Lectures That Have **Happened**

1. A chapter is written **after** its lecture, from the slides **and the recording**. The recording shows which slides the lecturer spent time on and which were skipped, and the chapter follows that.
2. Lectures still to come, or not written yet, are listed below so you can see exactly what's **missing**. :src[derived]

::edition

## Every Chapter Is **Checked** Before It Goes In

1. Each page **cites** the slide it came from (the small "Sources" line at the bottom). `D s5` means slide 5 of the slide deck; `EX p1` is the exercise sheet.
2. Sums on the pages are **recomputed** by automatic checks, where a check is linked, when the book is built.
3. A second, independent pass then compares every page with its sources. :src[derived]
